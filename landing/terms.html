Terms of Service

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

1. Platform Overview

ChargeBridge operates an intermediary technology platform connecting EV charger hosts with EV drivers seeking charging facilities. ChargeBridge charges a commission fee (typically 10-15%) on completed bookings.

2. Booking & Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellation > 2 hours prior to start: 100% refund to driver.
  • Cancellation between 30 minutes and 2 hours prior: 50% refund to driver.
  • Cancellation < 30 minutes prior: Non-refundable.
  • Host cancellation: 100% refund to driver; host receives an account penalty.

3. Host Electrical Safety Guidelines & Compliance

Mandatory Safety Compliance for Hosts

All hosts represent and warrant that their charging equipment, electrical wiring, earthing, and circuit breakers (MCB/RCCB) comply with national electrical standards (IS/IEC standards in India) and have been installed by a certified licensed electrician.

ChargeBridge does not own, inspect, or maintain individual host electrical installations and assumes no liability for electrical faults, equipment malfunction, or vehicular damage occurring on host premises.

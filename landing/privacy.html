Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register, book, or host on ChargeBridge, we collect:

  • Personal details: Name, email address, phone number.
  • Location information: Device GPS coordinates to show nearby chargers and charger location coordinates.
  • Payment information: Processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store raw credit card numbers.
  • Device data: FCM tokens for push notifications, audit log records including IP addresses.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to facilitate charger bookings, verify hosts and drivers, process payments and host payouts, provide push notifications, and ensure platform safety and compliance.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share only necessary details (e.g. charger address and host phone) with confirmed booking parties for session coordination.

4. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding privacy, please contact support@chargebridge.app.
